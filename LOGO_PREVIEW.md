## 🎯 Design Rationale

### Why Lightning Bolt?
- **Speed**: Represents fast optimization
- **Power**: Shows processing capability
- **Energy**: Dynamic and active
- **Transformation**: Converting images

### Why Purple-Pink Gradient?
- **Modern**: Trendy color combination
- **Tech**: Associated with innovation
- **Premium**: High-quality feel
- **Memorable**: Stands out in tabs

### Why Circular Background?
- **Professional**: Clean and polished
- **Recognizable**: Easy to identify
- **Scalable**: Works at any size
- **Friendly**: Approachable design
